#  Project Context

## Introduction

CarbonTrust Registry is an AI-powered web application designed to improve trust, transparency, and efficiency in the carbon credit issuance process for Blue Carbon ecosystems.

The platform enables NGOs to register restoration projects, submit MRV (Measurement, Reporting, and Verification) evidence, undergo verification by authorized verifiers, receive verified carbon credits, and make those credits available to buyers through a secure marketplace.

---

## What are Blue Carbon Ecosystems?

Blue Carbon ecosystems are coastal and marine environments that naturally absorb and store large amounts of carbon dioxide from the atmosphere.

Examples include:

- Mangrove Forests
- Seagrass Meadows
- Salt Marshes

These ecosystems play an important role in reducing climate change while also protecting biodiversity and coastal communities.

---

## Problem Statement

Although Blue Carbon ecosystems capture significant amounts of carbon, many restoration projects fail to receive carbon credits due to a lack of trust in the existing MRV (Measurement, Reporting, and Verification) process.

The current system often faces challenges such as:

- Manual verification processes
- Lack of transparency
- Difficulty validating uploaded evidence
- Slow approval process
- Limited trust among carbon credit buyers

Because of these issues, many genuine restoration projects struggle to receive recognition and financial support.

---

## Proposed Solution

CarbonTrust Registry provides a centralized digital platform where NGOs can manage the complete lifecycle of Blue Carbon restoration projects.

The platform allows:

- NGOs to register restoration projects
- Upload MRV evidence
- Verifiers to review submitted evidence
- Administrators to manage the platform
- Buyers to purchase verified carbon credits

The system improves transparency by maintaining a structured workflow from project registration to carbon credit issuance.

---

## Expected Outcome

The CarbonTrust Registry aims to:

- Increase trust in Blue Carbon projects
- Simplify the MRV process
- Improve transparency during verification
- Enable faster carbon credit issuance
- Encourage more organizations to invest in Blue Carbon conservation

---

## Project Vision

To build a secure, transparent, and scalable Carbon Credit Registry that supports environmental conservation while providing confidence to NGOs, verifiers, administrators, and carbon credit buyers.
